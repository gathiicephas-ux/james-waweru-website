About James

From Sales Executive to Group Head — A 23-Year Journey

James Waweru began his career in insurance in 2002 as a Sales Executive at Mediplus, driven by a belief that financial protection could transform lives across East Africa. His natural ability to build relationships and inspire teams saw him rise quickly through the ranks.

In 2016, James made history — becoming the first person on the African continent to qualify for the LIMRA Agency and Insurance Agency Management (AIAM) designation. That same year he was appointed Head of Direct Sales Force at APA Insurance, where he led the team to four AKI Company of the Year titles.

Today, as Group Head of Direct Sales Force at APA Apollo Group, James oversees distribution strategy across Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania — building one of East Africa's most decorated insurance sales organisations.
